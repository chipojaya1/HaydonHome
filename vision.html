01 — OUR VISION

Distinctive comfort.
Authentic Zimbabwean hospitality.

To establish HaydonHome as a distinguished guesthouse in Mt Hampden, offering exceptional Zimbabwean hospitality in an elegant, peaceful and welcoming environment where every guest feels at home.
